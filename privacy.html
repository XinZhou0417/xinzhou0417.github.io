Privacy

Last updated 23 August 2026

This page says what xzhou.au collects, why, how long it keeps it, and who else sees it. It describes what the code actually does.

Contact for anything on this page: xinzhou0417@gmail.com.

## Reading the site

The site is static, served from GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, embeds no tracking pixels and loads nothing from a third party. There is no advertising and nothing here is sold or shared with anyone.

## Asking the assistant a question

When you send a question, the following is written to a database on my own server in Australia, as one row:

  • the question and the answer,
  • which of the safety checks ran and what each decided,
  • how many tokens the answer cost and which model wrote it,
  • and a keyed hash of your IP address, never the address itself, so repeated use can be counted without anyone being identified from it.

All of it is deleted after 14 days. The code enforces that window and a test checks it.

The transcript you can see in the chat window is kept only in your own browser, for that tab, and disappears when you close it.

Before your first question the site runs Cloudflare Turnstile, a check that you are a person and not a script. Cloudflare handles that and I do not receive anything from it beyond pass or fail.

Please do not type anything into the box that you would mind me reading. It is a free-text field on a public page, and I do read them. That is how I find out where the assistant answers badly.

## Subscribing

If you subscribe, Stripe collects and holds your payment details. I never see them. Stripe acts as merchant of record through Link, and its own privacy terms apply to the payment.

I store four things:

  • your email address, which Stripe passes on,
  • your Stripe customer reference,
  • your subscription's status and current period,
  • and a running total of what you have used this period.

Your email address is the whole of your account. There is no password and no profile. All four are kept for as long as you have a subscription. Ask me and I will delete it, and you can also ask Stripe directly, in which case Stripe deletes its record and tells my service to erase mine.

Signing in sends a six-digit code to your email address, through Resend. The email contains no links, and nothing is tracked. No opens, no clicks. Resend handles the delivery and its own terms apply.

Being signed in sets one cookie, xz_ent. It identifies your session and nothing else. It holds no personal information, cannot be read by scripts, lasts thirty days, and is replaced each time it is used so that a copied cookie stops working. Signing out ends it immediately.

## Who else is involved

What they see
Anthropic the text of your question, to write an answer. Nothing that identifies you goes with it
Cloudflare traffic to the site, and the human check
Stripe / Link everything about a payment, as merchant of record
Resend your email address and the sign-in code
GitHub serves the static pages

Each is a company with its own privacy terms. I use them because building any of it myself would be worse for you.

## Your rights

Ask me for a copy of what I hold about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it, and I will. Anything I hold is small enough to send you in an email.

If you think I have handled something badly, tell me first. I would rather fix it. You can also complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.
