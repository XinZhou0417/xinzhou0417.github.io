$ man johnny

## NAME

johnny - the snitch in the corner. Answers questions about Xin.

## SYNOPSIS

$ ask johnny · then type a question, or one of the commands below.

## DESCRIPTION

Johnny is a multi-modal agent. He reads what you type, looks at pictures you attach, and listens when you speak. He picks his own tools when he needs them.

He answers questions about Xin: where Xin is, who he has worked for, what he has shipped, a few personal things Xin chose to share, and computational geometry, which Xin likes. He says where he got it, shows every check the answer went through on the way in and out, and can take a message and email it to Xin.

## COMMANDS

/help
The list, in the terminal.
/tools
Everything he can reach, and what each one needs first. Read off the service, so it cannot go stale.
/subscribe
An allowance of your own, or how much of yours is left.
/upload-image
Attach a picture to the next thing you send. Dragging one onto the window or pasting a screenshot does the same. PNG, JPEG, WEBP or GIF, up to 6 MB.
/unmute
Talk to him out loud. Your browser asks for the microphone; it is never requested before this.
/mic
Always listening, or hold the space bar to speak. Nothing to switch until /unmute has opened the microphone.
/mute
Stop the voice. Choose listening, where you speak and johnny types back, or text only, which listens to nothing.
/auth
Sign in on another device. Your email and six digits. No password.
/exit
End it. The transcript goes with him.

Commands never reach the model, so they work when nothing else does./unmute and/upload-image are the exceptions. Both start something that costs money, and both are metered like a question.

## TOOLS

He is not a search box with a personality. He decides what to do, does it, reads the result and decides again, and the terminal prints each step as it happens.

search his material
Looks through Xin’s published resume and site. Finding nothing is a normal answer, not a refusal, and he says so and moves on.
the personal ones
Favourite film, game, music, and the small details behind this site. Open to everyone.
leave a message
Say you want to reach Xin and he will take a name, a way to reply and your message, then read back the exact text he would send. Nothing goes anywhere until you agree to it, and he cannot send anything other than what he read back. Plain text only, and nothing is stored: the email is the only copy. Seeprivacy.

A picture is read by a second agent instead, with no tools and no memory of the conversation. It reports what it saw, and Johnny takes it from there.

## LIMITS

Two limits on questions. Each conversation gets a set number. The thinking he does each day comes out of one pot, shared by everybody who visits, and it refills at midnight.

He says which one you hit. A subscription has an allowance of its own, so it does not draw on that pot. What it covers.

Talking is the expensive part. A minute out loud costs about as much as ten typed questions, or roughly half that if you speak and he types back.

Pictures go one per message, or three at a time with a subscription.

## DATA

Three things, kept 14 days, then deleted: your question, his answer, and which checks ran. It is how Xin finds the ones Johnny gets wrong.

Your IP address is not kept. Only a one-way scramble of it, enough to notice someone hammering the door and not enough to point back at you.

Speak to him and the audio is not kept, on either side. What you said turns up in the input line first, so you read it and send it yourself, and from there it is a question like any other. The length of the call is recorded, not the sound of it.

A picture you attach is held in memory for ten minutes, never written to disk, and never served back out. What is kept is how many came with the question, never what was in them.

The question goes to the model that writes the answer, with nothing attached saying who asked. Nothing is sold or handed on.

Subscribe and he keeps your email too. That is the whole account. Stripe takes the card; Xin never sees it. Ask, and he deletes the lot.
