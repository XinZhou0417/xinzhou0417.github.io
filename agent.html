$ man johnny

Johnny works the corner outside every page on this site. He’ll tell you where Xin is, who he’s worked for, what he’s shipped and how to reach him — and he’ll point at where he got it, because a snitch without a source is just a gossip.

Ask him how any of it was built and he goes quiet. Not modesty — that part was never handed to him. Those ones are Xin’s, and Johnny’s happy to say where to find him.

Every question gets frisked on the way in and every answer on the way out. He shows the notes either way. When something gets turned away, that’s the part worth reading — he says which check stopped it, and why, instead of shrugging.

He is in the corner of this page, and every other one. Press $ ask johnny and put something to him.

If he clams up, ask Xin yourself — About.

What holds him back

Johnny answers a handful of questions per person, and does a fixed amount of thinking each day. Hit either and he tells you which — in amber, with what to do — instead of pretending nothing happened. When the day’s thinking is gone he still finds the right pages and cites them; he just stops writing prose until tomorrow.

He is a demonstration running on Xin’s own money, so those ceilings are the reason he can be open to anyone with no signup rather than behind a login.

What he keeps

Johnny keeps what you ask him for 14 days, then deletes it — your question, his answer, and which checks ran. It’s how Xin finds the ones Johnny gets wrong. Your IP address isn’t kept; only a one-way scramble of it, enough to notice someone hammering the door but not enough to point back at you. None of it goes anywhere else.
