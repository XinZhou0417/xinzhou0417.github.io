## The subscription

A subscription costs A$5.50 per month, including GST, charged monthly until cancelled. Payments are handled by Stripe, which acts as merchant of record through Link and collects the GST. I never see or hold your card details.

A subscription gives you a larger monthly usage allowance and a higher limit on the length of a single conversation. It also keeps the assistant answering you after the day's shared budget is spent, which is when it otherwise goes quiet for everybody. The current allowances are stated in the assistant itself. Type /subscribe to see them, and how much of yours remains. They are generous for ordinary use, and they are still ceilings.

Speaking to the assistant and sending it pictures both cost real money to run, so a subscription buys more of both.

Speaking. A subscription includes an allowance of talking time, metered in the same pot as everything else. You can also talk without one: there is a few minutes a day for every visitor, drawing on the day's shared budget. Speech costs a good deal more to run than typing, so that share is smaller than the typing one, and a subscription is more of it rather than the only way in.

Pictures. Without a subscription you can send one picture at a time, and a few a day. With one you can send several in the same message, and many more of them over a month.

The current allowances are stated in the assistant itself. They are numbers I expect to adjust, so they live where they can be kept true rather than in this document, and /subscribe shows both the limits and how much of yours remains.

You should still subscribe for what it does today rather than for what it might do next.

Usage is measured by what your questions cost to run, rather than by how many you send, because a spoken minute and a typed question do not cost the same. The allowance refills when the subscription renews. Unused allowance does not carry over.

I may change the allowances. If a change reduces what you have, I will tell subscribers before it takes effect. If I change the price, existing subscribers stay on the price they signed up at unless I tell them otherwise in advance.
